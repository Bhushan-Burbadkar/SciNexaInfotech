Industry Challenges

Why laboratory operations are difficult to manage at scale

Manual sample tracking, paper-based results entry, fragmented systems, and duplicate data entry create delays, errors, and weak audit trails in modern laboratory operations.

1

Manual Sample Tracking

Tracking samples manually across multiple locations and test stages creates bottlenecks and lost visibility.

2

Paper-Based Results Entry

Recording test results on paper and transcribing them is time-consuming and error-prone.

3

Disconnected Lab Systems

Multiple systems for different instruments and tests create data silos and integration gaps.

4

Slow Batch Release

Collecting results, performing calculations, and obtaining approvals manually delays batch release decisions.

5

Weak Audit Trails

Distributed records and manual processes make it difficult to provide complete audit evidence during inspections.
