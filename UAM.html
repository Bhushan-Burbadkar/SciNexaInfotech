Industry Challenges

Why centralized access management is difficult to maintain

Distributed user management, manual access requests, fragmented approvals, and incomplete audit trails create compliance gaps, access violations, and regulatory findings during inspections.

1

Distributed User Management

Managing users across multiple systems creates inconsistencies and compliance gaps.

2

Manual Access Requests

Paper-based or email access requests are slow, error-prone, and difficult to track.

3

Fragmented Approvals

Access approvals from multiple systems make it hard to track who approved what and when.

4

Incomplete Audit Trails

Limited audit evidence creates compliance risk when regulators ask to review access decisions.

5

Slow Onboarding/Offboarding

Manual access provisioning and removal delays new hire productivity and increases security risk.
