Industry Challenges

Why annual quality reviews are difficult to manage

Most companies still compile APQR data manually from multiple systems, making consolidation slow, error-prone, and vulnerable to incomplete historical context during audits.

1

Manual Data Consolidation

Gathering annual quality data from disparate systems is time-consuming and prone to errors and omissions.

2

Difficult Trend Analysis

Identifying meaningful trends requires manual analysis across multiple data sources and historical records.

3

Incomplete OOS Investigation Links

Out-of-spec deviations and their resolution history are often fragmented across local files and systems.

44

Regulatory Compliance Risk

Missing audit evidence or incomplete trend analysis can slow regulatory submissions and create compliance gaps.

5

Limited Historical Visibility

Auditors struggle to validate multi-year trends and quality decisions without centralized data records.
